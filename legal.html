Food Safety Registration

Mama's Table is a registered food business with Caerphilly County Borough Council Environmental Health Department, as required under the Food Safety Act 1990.

We hold a 5/5 Food Hygiene Rating as awarded by Caerphilly County Borough Council. Our rating is available to view on the Food Standards Agency website.

As a home-based food business, we are registered as a food establishment under Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 on the hygiene of foodstuffs.

Insurance

Mama's Table maintains public liability insurance appropriate for a home-based food business. This cover protects against third-party claims arising from our products or premises.

A copy of our current insurance certificate is available on request by contacting us via email or WhatsApp.
