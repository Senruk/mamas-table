# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in and around Caerphilly, South Wales looking for homemade celebration cakes, cupcakes, traybakes, and custom bakes for birthdays, weddings, events, and everyday treats.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing website for Mama's Table, a home baking business in Caerphilly. Its purpose is to showcase the baker's work, communicate the quality and care behind every bake, and convert visitors into orders. Success is measured by enquiries received through the order form.

## Brand Personality

Warm, traditional, homemade. The site should feel like you've walked into a genuine home kitchen — not a commercial bakery. Trustworthy (5/5 hygiene rating), personal (small-batch handmade), and rooted in traditional baking values.

## Anti-references

- No generic bakery template aesthetics (cupcake clipart, pink-and-teal colour schemes)
- Not corporate or sterile — should feel personal and handmade, not like a chain bakery

## Design Principles

1. **Made, not manufactured.** Everything about the site should feel handmade — the typography, the textures, the imagery. No stock-template polish.
2. **Show the food.** The bakes are the hero. Let the photography do the talking, keep layouts simple and uncluttered.
3. **Trust through transparency.** Display the 5/5 hygiene rating, registered kitchen status, and insurance details prominently. For a home business, trust is the conversion driver.
4. **Low-friction ordering.** The path from "this looks good" to "I'd like to order" should be as short as possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast)
- prefers-reduced-motion support for all animations
- Focus-visible outlines on all interactive elements
- Descriptive alt text on all images
