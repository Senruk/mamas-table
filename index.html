About Us

Your daily dose of taste and tradition

Mama's Table started from a simple love of baking. What began as a kitchen hobby (cakes for family, treats for friends) grew into something more. Word spread, orders followed, and today we bake for homes across Caerphilly and beyond.

Every cake, cupcake and traybake is made by hand, in small batches, using quality ingredients. No mixes, no shortcuts, just honest homemade baking.

Registered with Caerphilly council. 5/5 food hygiene rating. Fully insured.
